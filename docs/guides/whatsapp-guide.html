WhatsApp in your shop

Everything the website and the admin do with WhatsApp: what customers see, what the team uses every day, and how to do each task step by step.

Your shop's WhatsApp number: 0554 65 07 18 (set in the admin; every button follows it).

How it works: every WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp on the phone or computer with the message already written. Nothing is sent by itself: the person checks the message and taps send.

Inside: 1. Setup · 2. What customers see (11 places) · 3. What the team uses (10 tools) · 4. Step-by-step tasks · 5. Good practice and limits

1. Setup: one number for the whole shop

All the store's WhatsApp buttons use one number. Change it in one place and every button follows.

  1. Admin → Paramètres الإعدادات → the Contact & réseaux الاتصال والشبكات tab.
  2. Type the number in the WhatsApp field, the way you'd say it: 0554 65 07 18. A wa.me link also works.
  3. Enregistrer. The store shows the new number within a few minutes.
Leave the field empty and every WhatsApp button disappears from the store (useful when nobody can answer, for example during a holiday). The phone number and Instagram stay.

4. Step by step

4.1 Confirm a new order on WhatsApp

  1. A new order arrives in the Telegram group (or in Commandes → À confirmer).
  2. Tap 💬 WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens on her number with this message (in her language):

Bonjour Amina 🌸 Ici El Fatina. Nous confirmons votre commande EF-4K7P (5 400 DA), livraison à domicile à Alger. Merci !

  1. Check the message, send it, and wait for her answer.
  2. She agrees: tap ✅ Confirmer (in Telegram or in the order). No answer: 📵 Injoignable, and set a time to call back (Telegram reminds the team).
Calling first and sending WhatsApp only when she doesn't pick up works best: a call confirms faster and cuts fake orders.

4.2 Note a WhatsApp contact in the order's history

  1. Open the order in Commandes.
  2. Tap 💬 WhatsApp: a small box opens under the buttons.
  3. Add what she said (e.g. « préfère être livrée samedi ») and save. It appears in Historique with your name and the time.

4.3 Record an order received on WhatsApp

  1. Admin → Vente au magasin (or Ventes → « Vente manuelle »).
  2. Choose 💬 WhatsApp at the top.
  3. Tap the pieces and sizes, type her name and phone, choose the wilaya and home / office delivery.
  4. Tap the save button once. The order is created confirmed and the stock is taken off the website at once. A second tap by mistake does not create a second order.

4.4 A parcel couldn't be delivered

  1. Admin → Expéditions → 📞 Échecs de livraison.
  2. Tap 💬 WhatsApp next to the customer: the message asks when she is available.
  3. Write down her answer (new day, new address) and choose the result, e.g. « Nouveau passage demandé », then ask the courier for the new delivery.

4.5 Tell waiting customers that a size is back

  1. Put the stock back (Stock → Réception, or tap the number).
  2. Customers who turned on notifications are told automatically.
  3. The others: Notifier → the product's card → tap each number: the « est de retour » message is ready.
  4. When done: Marquer comme prévenues.

4.6 Answer a message from the contact page

  1. Admin → Contact: the newest messages first.
  2. If she left a phone number, tap WhatsApp to answer there; otherwise call or reply by phone.

4.7 Share a product or a promotion on WhatsApp (and know what it sells)

  1. Open the product on the website and tap Partager → WhatsApp (chat, group or Status). The link shows its photo and price.
  2. To measure what WhatsApp brings: Admin → Sources المصادر → next to WhatsApp, 📋 Lien de suivi copies the shop's link marked « WhatsApp ».
  3. Share that link (Status, groups). Visits and orders that come from it are counted under WhatsApp in Sources. For one campaign, add &utm_campaign=statut-aid at the end: it gets its own line.

4.8 Invite a team member with WhatsApp

  1. Admin → Équipe الفريق → + Inviter quelqu'un.
  2. Name, email, then choose her role card (Confirmatrice, Préparation / Stock, Marketing…).
  3. Créer l'invitation → 💬 Envoyer sur WhatsApp → choose her chat. The link works 7 days, once.

4.9 Change the shop's WhatsApp number

See part 1: Paramètres → Contact & réseaux → WhatsApp → Enregistrer.

5. Good practice and limits

Use WhatsApp Business (free) on the shop's phone: quick replies (« / » + shortcut) for answers you send often, labels (New order, Paid, To deliver), and away messages outside opening hours.

One phone, one number: the number in Paramètres should be a phone the team actually watches all day. A message that waits a day is often a lost sale.

Customers' privacy (law 18-07): never forward a customer's address, phone or screenshots to other groups. Delete chats of customers who ask you to erase their data (Admin → Clients → « 🧹 Effacer ses données »).

Fake orders: an order nobody answers on the phone or WhatsApp after 48 h holds stock for nothing. Commandes shows « Libérer le stock » for these: cancel them (reason « injoignable ») once the team has tried.

What the shop does NOT do by itself: it never sends WhatsApp messages automatically. Every message is opened, checked and sent by a person. Automatic messages (for example « your parcel has left ») would need the paid WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta: a verified business, approved message templates, and a cost for each conversation. It isn't set up, to stay on free tools.

Telegram stays the team's notification channel: new orders, reminders, the evening report.
